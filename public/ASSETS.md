# Graphics

The built-in procedural dwarf and terrain artwork in `app.js` and the vector preview in `docs/fortress.svg` are original Agent Fort artwork, distributed under the project's MIT license.

Optional `dwarves.png` and `geology.png` files are local overrides and are Git-ignored. They are not part of the public distribution and are not covered by this repository's license. See `docs/custom-graphics.md` for layout details.
